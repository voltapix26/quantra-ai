AI Agent Desk

Seven AI agents work any asset like a desk: four specialist analysts and three investor personas report in parallel, a bull and a bear debate the evidence, a head trader commits to a stance, and a risk manager gets the final veto. Research commentary — not investment advice.

Pick an asset and run the desk. A full run takes about a minute.

Architecture inspired by the open-source TradingAgents (Apache-2.0) and ai-hedge-fund (MIT) research projects, re-implemented natively on Quantra's own data and AI engine. Personas are archetypes, not real people. All output is AI-generated analytical commentary with no guarantee of accuracy and is not investment advice, a recommendation, or an offer to buy or sell any security.
